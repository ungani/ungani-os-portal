Business Sections

Your business type is —. Choose the sections that apply to your business. This controls your dashboard layout, task types, and money categories - you can change this any time.

Tax Settings

Turn this on if your business is VAT-registered with KRA. Once on, you can mark individual Money records as VAT-applicable and the system will track VAT payable for you. Withholding tax tracking is available on every transaction regardless of this setting.

Multi-Currency

Turn this on if your business also gets paid in US Dollars. Once on, Money records can be entered in either KES or USD - the USD amount is converted to a KES equivalent using that day's exchange rate at the moment you save, so every total, VAT/withholding figure, and report stays in KES. Rates are refreshed once a day and provided by ExchangeRate-API.com.

Google Drive Integration

Connect your business's Google Drive account once, then pick real files or folders straight from Drive when adding a document - no more pasting links by hand. UNGANI only ever gets access to files you specifically pick, never your whole Drive.
