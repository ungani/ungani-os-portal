This isn't a single click-to-pay button — it's a few steps:

  1. 1 Request your plan — choose a package below (or keep your current one) and submit.
  2. 2 UNGANI reviews it — admin checks your request.
  3. 3 You'll receive payment instructions — once approved, UNGANI will tell you exactly how to pay (e.g. M-Pesa Paybill or bank transfer).
  4. 4 Upload your proof of payment — on the Billing page, once you've paid.
  5. 5 UNGANI confirms it — your account is reactivated.
