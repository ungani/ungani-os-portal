How This Works

UNGANI support never needs, sees, or uses your password.
  • Invite UNGANI Support flags that you'd like help - no access is granted yet.
  • Grant Temporary Access is what actually turns access on, for the level and duration you choose.
  • Support staff sign in with their own UNGANI login - your grant is what lets them in, not a shared password.
  • Access ends automatically when it expires, or the moment you click Revoke - whichever comes first.
  • Every invite, grant, revoke, and support session is recorded in the history below and in your account's audit trail.
  • UNGANI Support Access never counts as one of your team seats - it's entirely separate from your Team Access user limit.
